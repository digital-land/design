Conservation areas are defined by the [Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990][legislation:ukpga/1990/9#section-69] as areas of special architectural or historic interest, the character or appearance of which it is desirable to preserve or enhance.

They are a consideration for a number of different planning decisions related to [conserving and enhancing the historic environment][govuk:guidance/conserving-and-enhancing-the-historic-environment].

The dataset contains some authoritative data provided by local planning authorities, 
as well as data collected from from Historic England and other sources found on
[data.gov.uk](https://www.data.gov.uk/search?q=conservation+area). 
It should be treated as a work in progress, and is currently incomplete.
It also contains a number of duplicate conservation areas we are working to reconcile.
