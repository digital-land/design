# Amendment to permitted development rights for petroleum exploration

This consultation seeks views on the government’s proposal to amend Part 22 of the [Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) Order 1995](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/418/schedule/2/made) to allow the drilling of boreholes for groundwater monitoring for petroleum exploration.

This change would enable works to be carried out to establish baseline information on the groundwater environment in advance of, or in parallel to, any planning application(s) coming forward for such development.
