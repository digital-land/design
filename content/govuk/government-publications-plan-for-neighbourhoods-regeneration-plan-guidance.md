# Plan for Neighbourhoods: Regeneration Plan guidance

As part of the Plan for Neighbourhoods programme, places must create a Regeneration Plan outlining their overarching vision for change over the next 10 years to deliver the strategic objectives of the programme.

[Plan for Neighbourhoods: 10-year vision content checklist](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/plan-for-neighbourhoods-regeneration-plan-guidance/plan-for-neighbourhoods-10-year-vision-content-checklist) outlines the content you must include in your 10-year vision.
