# The Town and Country Planning (Consultation) (England) Direction 2024

On the 26 January 2024 a revised Town and Country Planning (Consultation) (England) Direction 2024 was published in response to Section 136 of the [Levelling up and Regeneration Act 2023](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55/contents/enacted).
