[ancient woodland](term/ancient-woodland) is any area that’s been wooded continuously since at least 1600 AD. It includes:
* [ancient semi-natural woodland](term/ancient-semi-natural-woodland) mainly made up of trees and shrubs native to the site, usually arising from natural regeneration
* [plantations](term/plantation) on ancient woodland sites - replanted with conifer or broadleaved trees that retain ancient woodland features, such as undisturbed soil, ground flora and fungi
They have equal protection in the [National Planning Policy Framework](term/national-planning-policy-framework) ([NPPF](abbreviation/NPPF)).
Other distinct forms of ancient woodland are:
* wood pastures identified as ancient
* [histoic parkland](term/historic-parkland), which is protected as a heritage asset in the [NPPF](abbreviaton/NPPF)
* Many of these do not appear on the [ancient woodland inventory](term/ancient-woodland-inventory) ([AWI](abbreviation/AWI)) because their low tree density does not register as woodland on historic maps.
