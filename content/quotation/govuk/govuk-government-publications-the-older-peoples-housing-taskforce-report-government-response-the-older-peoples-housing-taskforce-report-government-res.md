A means test ensures this funding is focused on those who can least afford to adapt their home, although local authorities can agree a more flexible local policy on means testing and funding depending on local need. Following [consultation](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/changing-the-way-government-allocates-disabled-facilities-grant-funding-to-local-authorities-in-england/outcome/changing-the-way-government-allocates-disabled-facilities-grant-funding-to-local-authorities-in-england-consultation-response) in 2025, a new approach to allocating Disabled Facilities Grant funding to local authorities in England from 2026-27 will ensure the grant is better aligned with local needs.
