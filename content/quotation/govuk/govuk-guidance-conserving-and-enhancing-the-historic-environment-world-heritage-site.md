World Heritage Sites are defined as designated heritage assets in the [National Planning Policy Framework](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-planning-policy-framework--2). The [National Planning Policy Framework][term:national-planning-policy-framework] sets out detailed policies for the conservation and enhancement of the [historic environment][term:historic-environment], including World Heritage Sites, through both plan-making and decision-making.
