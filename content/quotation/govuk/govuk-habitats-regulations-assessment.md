A [Habitats Regulations Assessment][term:habitats-regulations-assessment] ([HRA][abbreviation:hra]) is a mandatory process that first tests if a proposed plan or project could have a likely significant effect on the designated features of a protected [European site][term:european-site] or [Ramsar site][term:ramsar-site].
