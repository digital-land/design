In this section the expression “local authority”, in relation to a parish, includes a parish meeting and the representative body of a parish, and the expressions “charitable purposes”, “[charity][term:charity]”, “charity trustees”, “court” and “trusts” have the same meanings as in the Charities Act 1960.
