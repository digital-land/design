“[owner][term:owner]”, in relation to any land, means a person, other than a mortgagee not in possession, who is for the time being entitled to dispose of the fee simple in [the land][term:the-land], whether in possession or in reversion, and includes also a person holding or entitled to the rents and profits of [the land][term:the-land] under a lease the unexpired term of which exceeds 3 years.
