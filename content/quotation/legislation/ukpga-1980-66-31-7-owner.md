For the purposes of the foregoing provisions of this section “[owner][term:owner]”, in relation to any land, means a person who is for the time being entitled to dispose of the fee simple in [the land][term:the-land]; and for the purposes of subsections (5) , (6), (6C) and (13) “the appropriate council” means the council of the county , metropolitan district or [London borough][term:london-borough] in which the way (in the case of subsection (5)) or [the land][term:the-land] (in the case of subsections (6), (6C) and (13)) is situated or, where the way or land is situated in the City, the Common Council.
