For the purposes of this Part, in relation to land in England and Wales which is common land subject to rights of common (within the meaning of the Commons Act 2006), “[occupier](term/occupier)” includes the commoners or any of them; and

- (a) “common land” means common land as defined in section 22 of the Commons Registration Act 1965; and

- (b) “commoner” means a person with rights of common as defined in that section.
