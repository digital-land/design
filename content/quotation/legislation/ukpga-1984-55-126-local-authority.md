“[local authority][term:local-authority]” means the council of a district or [London borough][term:london-borough], the Common Council of the City of London, the Sub-Treasurer of the Inner Temple, the Under Treasurer of the Middle Temple or, for the purposes of Parts I and II above and of this Part so far as it relates to them, the Council of the Isles of Scilly but, in relation to Wales, means the council of a county or county borough;;
