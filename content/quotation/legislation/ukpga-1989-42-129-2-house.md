For the purposes of subsection (1) above, “[house][term:house]” includes premises which were originally constructedas a house but which, by the time the group repair scheme is prepared, havebeen divided so as to form one or more flats, with or without other premises;and, in the case of a house which has been so divided, the cost of such of theexternal works as relate to the house shall be apportioned between the severalparts into which the house has been divided in such way as may be agreedbetween the persons with owner’s interests in those parts or, in default ofagreement, equally.
