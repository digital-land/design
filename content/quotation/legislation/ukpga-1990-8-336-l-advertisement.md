“[advertisement][term:advertisement]” means any word, letter, model, sign, placard, board, notice awning, blind, device or representation, whether illuminated or not, in the nature of, and employed wholly or partly for the purposes of, advertisement, announcement or direction, and (without prejudice to the previous provisions of this definition) includes any hoarding or similar structure used or designed, or adapted for use and anything else principally used, or designed or adapted principally for use,, for the display of advertisements, and references to the display of advertisements shall be construed accordingly;
