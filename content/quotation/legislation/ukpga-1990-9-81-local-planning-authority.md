In this Act “[local planning authority][term:local-planning-authority]” means a [local planning authority][term:local-planning-authority] for an area in England and shall be construed in accordance with Part I of the principal Act and Schedule 4 to this Act (which makes further provision as to the exercise of functions under this Act).
