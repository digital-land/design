“[watercourse][term:watercourse]” includes all rivers, streams, ditches, drains, cuts, culverts, dykes, sluices, sewers and passages through which water flows except mains and other pipes which belong to the Environment Agency , the NRBW or a water undertaker or are used by a water undertaker or any other person for the purpose only of providing a supply of water to any premises.
