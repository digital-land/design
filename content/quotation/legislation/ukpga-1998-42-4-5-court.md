In this section “[court][term:court]” means—

- (a) the Supreme Court;

- (b) the Judicial Committee of the Privy Council;

- (c) the Court Martial Appeal Court;

- (d) in Scotland, the High Court of Justiciary sitting otherwise than as a trial court or the Court of Session;

- (e) in England and Wales or Northern Ireland, the High Court or the Court of Appeal.

- (f) the Court of Protection, in any matter being dealt with by the President of the Family Division, the President of the Business and Property Division or a puisne judge of the High Court.
