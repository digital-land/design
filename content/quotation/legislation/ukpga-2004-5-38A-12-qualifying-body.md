“**[qualifying body][term:qualifying-body]**” means a parish council, or an organisation or body designated as a [neighbourhood forum][term:neighbourhood-forum], authorised for the purposes of a neighbourhood development plan to act in relation to a [neighbourhood area][term:neighbourhood-area] as a result of section 61F of the principal Act, as applied by section 38C of this Act.
