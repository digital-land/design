“[Public authority][term:public-authority]” means any of the following—

- (a) a Minister of [the Crown][term:the-crown];

- (b) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

- (c) a public body (including a [government department][term:government-department], a local authority and a [local planning authority][term:local-planning-authority]);

- (d) a person holding an office—

 - (i) under [the Crown][term:the-crown],

 - (ii) created or continued in existence by a public general Act, or

 - (iii) the remuneration in respect of which is paid out of money provided by Parliament;

- (e) a statutory undertaker.
