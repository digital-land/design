In this section, “[local planning authority][term:local-planning-authority]” has the meaning given by—

- (a) section 37 of PCPA 2004 in relation to England, except that a Mayoral [development corporation][term:development-corporation] is a [local planning authority][term:local-planning-authority] for the purposes of this section only if it is the [local planning authority][term:local-planning-authority] for all purposes of Part 2 of PCPA 2004 in respect of the whole of its area and all kinds of development, and

- (b) section 78 of PCPA 2004 in relation to Wales.
