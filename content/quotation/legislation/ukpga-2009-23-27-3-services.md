For the purposes of this section, “[services][term:services]” includes, in particular, anything done under—

- (a) section 2(11) (provision of copy of guidance);

- (b) section 24(2) or (2B) (making available the results of research);

- (c) section 25(2), (3)(a) , (3A), (4) or (5) (advice, assistance and training facilities);

- (d) section 26 (information).
