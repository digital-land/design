For the purposes of this section and the operation for those purposes of section 1162 of, and Schedule 7 to, the Companies Act 2006 “[undertaking](term/undertaking)” means—

- (a) an undertaking as defined by section 1161(1) of the 2006 Act, or

- (b) a charity which is not an undertaking as so defined.
