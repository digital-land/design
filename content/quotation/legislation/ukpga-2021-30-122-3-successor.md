In subsection [(2)(b)](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/30/section/122/2/b) “[successor][term:successor]” (in relation to the landowner under the covenant) means a person who holds, in respect of any of [the land][term:the-land] to which the obligation relates—

- (a) the qualifying estate, or

- (b) an estate in land derived (whether immediately or otherwise) from the qualifying estate after the creation of the covenant.
