“[manufacturer][term:manufacturer]” has the meaning given by regulations under section 74[(1)](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/30/section/74/1) which may define that term by reference (in particular) to—

- a person’s involvement in the manufacture of a relevant product, or
- a person’s relationship with a person involved in the manufacture of a relevant product;
