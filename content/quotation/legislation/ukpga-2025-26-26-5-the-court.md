“**the court**” means a court having jurisdiction to make an order for possession of a dwelling-house let on an assured tenancy (see [section 40](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/50/section/40) of the [Housing Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/50));
