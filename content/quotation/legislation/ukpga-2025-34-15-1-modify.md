The power conferred by [section 14](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/34/section/14)[(1)](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/34/section/14/1) to “[modify][term:modify]” includes power to amend, add to or remove (and, in particular, includes power to make a person a party to an agreement or to discharge a party from its obligations under an agreement); and references in [section 14](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/34/section/14), [this section](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/34/section/15) and [section 16](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/34/section/16) to modification are to be construed accordingly.
