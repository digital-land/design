“[planning application][term:planning-application]” means—

- an application for [planning permission][term:planning-permission] under Part 3 or Part 13 of the [Town and Country Planning Act 1990][legislation:ukpga/1990/8],
- an application for [planning permission][term:planning-permission] under Part 3 or Part 12 of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997,
- an application for an order granting [development consent][term:development-consent] under section 37 of the [Planning Act 2008][legislation:ukpga/2008/29], or
- an application under section 36 or 36C of the Electricity Act 1989 (consent for construction etc of generating station);
