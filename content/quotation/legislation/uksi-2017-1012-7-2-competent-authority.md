In the following provisions (and as provided in regulation 69(3)(a)), “[competent authority][term:competent-authority]” includes the Scottish Ministers—

- (a) regulation 70(2), in so far as that paragraph relates to a deemed grant of [planning permission][term:planning-permission] under—

 - (i) section 57(2), (2A) and (2ZA) of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997 , to which regulation 70(1)(e)(ii) and (f) relate; or

 - (ii) section 5(1) of the Pipe-lines Act 1962 , to which regulation 70(1)(e)(iii) relates;

- (b) Chapters 4 and 5 of Part 6.
