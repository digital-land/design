“**[protected site][term:protected-site]**” means—

- a site within the national site network, all or part of which is in the UK marine area;
- a protected marine area;
- a [Ramsar site][term:ramsar-site], all or part of which is in the UK marine area; or
- an area notified as a [site of special scientific interest][term:site-of-special-scientific-interest] under section 28(1) of the [Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981][legislation:ukpga/1981/69] or section 3(1) of the Nature Conservation (Scotland) Act 2004, all or part of which is in the UK marine area;
