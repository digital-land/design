In paragraph (3) “[lawfully][term:lawfully]” means—

- (a) without contravention of Part 1 of the [Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981][legislation:ukpga/1981/69], the Wildlife (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, the Offshore Marine Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 2007 or these Regulations; or

- (b) in the case of a bird, egg or other thing killed, taken or sold in Gibraltar or a member State ..., without contravention (as the case may be) of the law of Gibraltar or the law of that State implementing the Wild Birds Directive.
