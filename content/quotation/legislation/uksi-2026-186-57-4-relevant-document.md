In this regulation, “[relevant document][term:relevant-document]” means the proposed minerals and [waste plan][term:waste-plan] document in relation to which a minerals and waste planning authority have sought observations and advice under section 15CA(3) of [the Act][term:the-act] and [regulation 56](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/186/regulation/56/made) (gateway 3: prescribed requirements assessment) or, where applicable, [regulation 58](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/186/regulation/58/made) (repeat of gateway 3).
