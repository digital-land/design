Where arrangements are in force under [section 101](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/70/section/101) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/70/section/101) [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/70/section/101) of the [1972 Act][legislation:ukpga/1972/70] for an application made to a [relevant local planning authority][term:relevant-local-planning-authority] (“A”) to be determined by another relevant [local planning authority][term:local-planning-authority] (“[B][term:b]”), these Regulations apply in relation to that application as though it had been made to B instead of A.
