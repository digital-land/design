A site for residential, commercial or mixed-use development which would be implemented in multiple phases, have significant infrastructure requirements and require a masterplan. Typically, for a residential-led development, strategic sites would have capacity for at least 1,500 dwellings (although the scale of residential development can vary depending on the mix of uses on the site).
