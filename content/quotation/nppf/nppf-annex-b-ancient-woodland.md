An area that has been wooded continuously since at least 1600 AD. It includes ancient semi-natural woodland ([ASNW][abbreviation:asnw]), plantations on [ancient woodland][term:ancient-woodland] sites (PAWS), ancient wood pasture and parkland (AWPP) and infilled ancient wood pasture and parkland (IAWPP).
