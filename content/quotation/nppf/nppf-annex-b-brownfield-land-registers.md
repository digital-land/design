Registers of [previously developed land][term:previously-developed-land] that local planning authorities consider to be appropriate for residential development, having regard to criteria in the Town and Country Planning ([Brownfield Land Registers][term:brownfield-land-registers]) Regulations 2017. Local planning authorities will be able to trigger a grant of [permission in principle][term:permission-in-principle] for residential development on suitable sites in their registers where they follow the required procedures.
