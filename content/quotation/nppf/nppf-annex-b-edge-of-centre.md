For retail purposes, a location that is well-connected to, and up to 300 metres from, a [primary shopping area][term:primary-shopping-area] (if no [primary shopping area][term:primary-shopping-area] has been defined in the development plan, it should be regarded as a location that is well-connected to, and up to 300 metres from, that part of the [town centre][term:town-centre] within which retail uses predominate – measured from its outer edge). For all other [main town centre uses][term:main-town-centre-uses], a location within 300 metres of a [town centre][term:town-centre] boundary. For office development, this includes locations outside the town centre but within 500 metres of a public transport interchange. In determining whether a site falls within the definition of [edge of centre][term:edge-of-centre], account should be taken of local circumstances.
