Any site which would be included within the definition at regulation 8 of the [Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017][legislation:uksi/2017/1012] and also including candidate [Special Areas of Conservation][term:special-areas-of-conservation] or [Special Protection Areas][term:special-protection-areas], listed or proposed [Ramsar sites][term:ramsar-sites], relevant Marine Sites, and compensatory habitats which relate to these sites[[footnote 70]](#footnote70).
