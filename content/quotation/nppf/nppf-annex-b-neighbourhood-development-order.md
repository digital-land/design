An Order made by a [local planning authority][term:local-planning-authority] (under the [Town and Country Planning Act 1990][legislation:ukpga/1990/8]) through which parish councils and neighbourhood forums can grant [planning permission][term:planning-permission] for a specific development proposal or classes of development.
