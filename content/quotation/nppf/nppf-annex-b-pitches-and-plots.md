‘Pitch’ means a pitch on a gypsy and traveller site and ‘plot’ means a pitch on a [travelling showpeople][term:travelling-showpeople] site (often called a ‘yard’). This terminology differentiates between residential pitches for [gypsies and travellers][term:gypsies-and-travellers] and mixed-use plots for [travelling showpeople][term:travelling-showpeople], which may / will need to incorporate space or to be split to allow for the storage of equipment.
