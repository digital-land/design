In this regulation “[publish](term/publish)” means make available to the public, or to any section of the public, in whatever form and by whatever means (including, in particular, by inclusion in any programme included in a programme service within the meaning of the Broadcasting Act 1990) ; and “publication” is to be construed accordingly.
