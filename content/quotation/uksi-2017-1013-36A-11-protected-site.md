“**protected site**” means—

- a site within the national site network, all or part of which is in the UK marine area;
- a protected marine area;
- a Ramsar site, all or part of which is in the UK marine area; or
- an area notified as a site of special scientific interest under section 28(1) of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 or section 3(1) of the Nature Conservation (Scotland) Act 2004, all or part of which is in the UK marine area;
