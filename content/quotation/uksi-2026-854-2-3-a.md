Where arrangements are in force under [section 101](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/70/section/101) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/70/section/101) [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/70/section/101) of the [1972 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/70/contents) for an application made to a relevant local planning authority (“[A](term/a)”) to be determined by another relevant local planning authority (“B”), these Regulations apply in relation to that application as though it had been made to B instead of A.
