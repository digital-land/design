# Areas exempt from office to residential change of use permitted development right 2013

New permitted development rights will allow change of use from offices to homes to provide new homes in existing buildings.

The maps above numbered 1.1 to 1.36 show the areas exempt from the office to residential change of use permitted development right 2013. These are copies. The true maps are signed by a member of the senior civil service and are kept at the office of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government and can be made available for inspection upon request.
