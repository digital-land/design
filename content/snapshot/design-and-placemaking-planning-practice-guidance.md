# Design and Placemaking Planning Practice Guidance

This consultation seeks views on the usability of the draft consolidated Design and Placemaking Planning Practice Guidance.

A draft version of the guidance has been published alongside this consultation. Both documents should be read together.

We are also consulting on [changes to the National Planning Policy Framework](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/national-planning-policy-framework-proposed-reforms-and-other-changes-to-the-planning-system).
