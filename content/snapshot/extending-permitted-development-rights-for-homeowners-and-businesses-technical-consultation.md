# Extending permitted development rights for homeowners and businesses: technical consultation

This consultation proposes changes to increase permitted development rights for extensions to homes and business premises in non-protected areas.

The government is consulting on a set of proposals to amend the [Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) Order 1995](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/418/contents/made) to allow homeowners and businesses to make larger extensions to their homes and business premises without requiring a planning application, and to allow quicker installation of broadband infrastructure.
