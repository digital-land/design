# External review of government planning practice guidance

On [21 December 2012](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/planning-practice-guidance-should-be-simpler) the government welcomed the report of the review of planning practice guidance led by Lord Taylor of Goss Moor, and announced a [short consultation](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/review-of-planning-practice-guidance) on the review group’s recommendations.

The review group’s report makes recommendations as to the scope and form of practice guidance that should be provided in future by government to support effective planning; what new or updated practice guidance should be published, with clear priorities; and what guidance should be cancelled.
