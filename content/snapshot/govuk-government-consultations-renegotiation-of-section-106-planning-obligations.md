# Renegotiation of Section 106 planning obligations

The government’s housing strategy [Laying the foundations: a housing strategy for England](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/laying-the-foundations-a-housing-strategy-for-england--2) published on 21 November 2011 set out a proposal to allow reconsideration of planning obligations agreed in more buoyant market conditions.

The aim of this consultation is set out the details of how the proposal will work and to seek views.
