# The Older People's Housing Taskforce Report: government response

This response sets out the government’s position on the recommendations made by the [Older People’s Housing Taskforce Report](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-older-peoples-housing-taskforce-report).

It highlights the government’s vision for housing for older people, the commitments already underway across government and the next steps to achieve the vision.
