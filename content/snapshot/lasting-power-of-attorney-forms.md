# Lasting power of attorney forms

You can download the forms and guidance on this page to make a lasting power of attorney (LPA) for decisions about:

- finances and property
- health and welfare

You can get:

- a complete pack of documents for each type of LPA
- individual forms and guidance

Make sure the page size on your printer settings is set to ‘A4’ or ‘Fit’

You can also read guidance on how to [avoid errors when completing a lasting power of attorney form](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/avoiding-errors-when-completing-a-lasting-power-of-attorney-form).

Fill in the forms and send them to the Office of the Public Guardian (OPG) at the address shown on the forms.

You can also [make an LPA online](https://www.lastingpowerofattorney.service.gov.uk/home).

If you’ve downloaded an LPA form (LP1F or LP1H) from this page, you use the same form both to make your LPA and to register your LPA.

However, you need to [download a separate registration form](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/register-a-lasting-power-of-attorney) if your LPA was made using one of the following forms:

- LPA114 and LPA117 – signed and dated correctly by 1 January 2016
- LP PW and LP PA – signed and dated correctly by 1 April 2011

## Alternative formats

You can email [customerservices@publicguardian.gov.uk](mailto:customerservices@publicguardian.gov.uk) to get any document on this page in an alternative format, such as:

- braille
- audio

Include your address, the title of the document you need and the format you need it in.
