# National policy statement for waste water: a framework document for planning decisions February 2012

This document contains the following information: National policy statement for waste water: a framework document for planning decisions on nationally significant waste water infrastructure.

This Command Paper was laid before Parliament by a Government Minister by Command of Her Majesty. Command Papers are considered by the Government to be of interest to Parliament but are not required to be presented by legislation.
