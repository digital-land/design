# Permitted development rights: response to the Select Committee report

The Levelling Up, Housing and Communities Select Committee undertook an inquiry in 2021 on permitted development rights. Their [July 2021 report](https://committees.parliament.uk/work/1131/permitted-development-rights/publications/) made a number of recommendations in respect of permitted development rights, Article 4 directions and use classes etc.

Alongside the introduction of the Levelling Up and Regeneration Bill and the government response to the Select Committee report on The Future of Planning, the government has now published its response to these recommendations.
