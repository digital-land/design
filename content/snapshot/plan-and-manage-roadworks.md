# Plan and manage roadworks

‘Street Manager’ must be used to:

- apply for street and road work permits
- assess permits
- record inspections
- add reinstatements (after work has been completed)

You must hold or [create an account to log into the service](https://department-for-transport-streetmanager.github.io/street-manager-docs/registration/).

[Access Street Manager >](https://www.manage-roadworks.service.gov.uk/)

## Who is eligible to use this service?

To use the ‘Street Manager’ service you must be a:

- highway authority
- utility company
- contractor carrying out works on behalf of a highway authority or utility company

Charges apply for highway authorities and utility companies.

## Viewing roadworks data

The roadworks data added to this system by companies is available for public viewing free of charge [via application programming interface and registration](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/find-and-use-roadworks-data).
