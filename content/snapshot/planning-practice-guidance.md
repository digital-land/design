# Planning practice guidance

The National Planning Policy Framework sets out the government’s planning policies for England and how these are expected to be applied. The most recent published version of this can be accessed at [National Planning Policy Framework](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-planning-policy-framework).

It was originally published on 27 March 2012 and revised in 2018, 2019, 2021, 2023 and 17 August 2026.
