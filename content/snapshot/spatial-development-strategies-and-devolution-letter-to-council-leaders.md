# Spatial development strategies and devolution: letter to council leaders

The government is launching a [consultation on sensible geographies for Spatial Development Strategies](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/areas-for-producing-spatial-development-strategies).

In parallel, it is inviting councils across England who do not already have an existing devolution agreement to express their interest in being part of the next wave of devolution in creating a Foundation Strategic Authority.

This is the letter sent to councils to announce this.
