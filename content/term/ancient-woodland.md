An [area](term/area) that has been [wooded](term/wooded) continuously since at least 1600 AD.
It includes [ancient semi-natural woodland](term/ancient-semi-natural-woodland) ([ASNW](abbreviation/ASNW),
[plantations on ancient woodland sites](term/plantations-on-ancient-woodland-sites) ([PAWS](abbreviation/PAWS)),
[ancient wood pasture and parkland](term/ancient-wood-pasture-and-parkland) ([AWPP](abbreviation/AWPP)), and
[infilled ancient wood pasture and parkland](term/infilled-ancient-wood-pasture-and-parkland) ([IAWPP](abbreviation/IAWPP)).
