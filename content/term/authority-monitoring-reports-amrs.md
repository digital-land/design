plural: Authority monitoring reports (amrs)

entry-date: '2026-11-13'
---

Local planning authorities must publish information at least annually, monitoring their progress on various planning activities, such as Local Plan preparation, activities relating to the duty to cooperate, indicators in the plan, and poligies which are not being implemented.
