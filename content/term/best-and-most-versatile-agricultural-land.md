Land in grades 1, 2 and 3a of the [Agricultural Land Classification][term:agricultural-land-classification] ([ALC][abbreviation:alc]). [ALC][abbreviation:alc] is graded from 1 to 5 and based on yield, range and versatility of use, and need for input.
