entry-date: 2026-11-12
---

Purpose built housing that is typically 100% rented out. It can form part of a wider multi-tenure development comprising either flats or houses, but should be on the same site and/or contiguous with the main development. Schemes will typically be professionally managed stock in single ownership and management control.
