reference: coastal-change-management-area
name: Coastal Change Management Area
term-status: guidance
documentation-url: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-planning-policy-framework-annex-b-glossary
entry-date: 2026-11-12
---

An area identified in plans as likely to be affected by physical changes to the coast, estuaries or tidal rivers, over the next century, through erosion, coastal landslip, permanent inundation or coastal accretion.
