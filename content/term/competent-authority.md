In these Regulations, “competent authority” means—

- (a) a Minister of [the Crown][term:the-crown], [government department][term:government-department], public or statutory undertaker, or public body of any description or person holding a public office;

- (b) the Scottish Ministers;

- (c) the Welsh Ministers;

- (d) any Northern Ireland department; and

- (e) any person exercising any function of a person or body referred to in sub-paragraphs (a) to (d).
