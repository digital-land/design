Is defined in section 38 of the [Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004][legislation:ukpga/2004/5], and includes adopted spatial development strategies, adopted local plans, adopted [minerals and waste plans][term:minerals-and-waste-plans], adopted [supplementary plans][term:supplementary-plan], neighbourhood plans that have been made and published and the policies maps for the area. Neighbourhood plans that have been approved at referendum are also part of the development plan, unless the [local planning authority][term:local-planning-authority] decides that the [neighbourhood plan][term:neighbourhood-plan] should not be made.
