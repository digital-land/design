name: Freight and logistics
term-status: guidance
documentation-url: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-planning-policy-framework-annex-b-glossary
entry-date: 2026-11-12
---

Uses directly associated with the processing, storage, distribution and transportation of goods, alongside supporting infrastructure. This includes (but is not limited to) storage and distribution operations, rail freight terminals, intermodal facilities, HGV operating centres, lorry parking facilities, ports and inland border facilities.
