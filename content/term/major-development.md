For housing, development where 10 or more homes will be provided, or the site has an area of 0.5 hectares or more. For non-residential development it means additional floorspace of 1,000m2 or more, or a site of 1 hectare or more, or as otherwise provided in the [Town and Country Planning (Development Management Procedure) (England) Order 2015](legislation/uksi-2015-595).
