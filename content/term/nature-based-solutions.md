---
reference: nature-based-solutions
name: Nature based solutions
term-status: guidance
documentation-url: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-planning-policy-framework-annex-b-glossary
entry-date: 2026-11-12
---

Actions which use natural processes to protect, sustainably manage, and restore natural and modified ecosystems that address societal challenges, benefiting both people and nature, such as the creation of woodlands or wetlands.
