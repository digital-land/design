A condition imposed on a grant of [planning permission][term:planning-permission] (in accordance with the [Town and Country Planning Act 1990][legislation:ukpga/1990/8]) or a condition included in a [Local Development Order][term:local-development-order], Mayoral Development Order or [Neighbourhood Development Order][term:neighbourhood-development-order].
