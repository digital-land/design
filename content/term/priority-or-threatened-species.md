Species which are particularly vulnerable to the impacts of development, including but not limited to species contained in the list of species and habitats of principal importance in England published by the Secretary of State pursuant to section 41 of the [Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006][legislation:ukpga/2006/16], protected species listed in Schedule 2 and Schedule 5 of the [Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017][legislation:uksi/2017/1012], and species protected by the [Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981][legislation:ukpga/1981/69].
