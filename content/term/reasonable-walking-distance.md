For the purpose of policies [S5](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-planning-policy-framework-chapter-4-achieving-sustainable-development-s1-6#s5-principle-of-development-outside-settlements), [L3](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-planning-policy-framework-chapter-12-making-effective-use-of-land-l1-3#l3-achieving-appropriate-densities), [GB7](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-planning-policy-framework-chapter-13-protecting-green-belt-land-gb1-8#gb7-development-which-is-not-inappropriate-in-the-green-belt) (relating to land around well-connected stations), this should be considered to be around 800 metres, or around 10 minutes’ walk time if topography, route availability and quality or physical barriers would prevent or discourage walking from up to 800 metres away. For the purpose of policy [HC5](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-planning-policy-framework-chapter-16-promoting-healthy-communities-hc1-8#hc5-hot-food-takeaways) (hot food takeaways) it should be considered to be around 400 metres, or around five minutes’ walk time if topography, route availability and quality or physical barriers would prevent or discourage walking up to 400 metres. Where a reasonable walking distance is required to be calculated when applying policies [S5(1)(h)](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-planning-policy-framework-chapter-4-achieving-sustainable-development-s1-6#s5-principle-of-development-outside-settlements), [L3(2)(c)](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-planning-policy-framework-chapter-12-making-effective-use-of-land-l1-3#l3-achieving-appropriate-densities) and [GB7(1)(h)](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-planning-policy-framework-chapter-13-protecting-green-belt-land-gb1-8#gb7-development-which-is-not-inappropriate-in-the-green-belt), and only part of the site falls within the reasonable walking distance, those sub-paragraphs of policies [S5](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-planning-policy-framework-chapter-4-achieving-sustainable-development-s1-6#s5-principle-of-development-outside-settlements), [L3](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-planning-policy-framework-chapter-12-making-effective-use-of-land-l1-3#l3-achieving-appropriate-densities) and [GB7](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-planning-policy-framework-chapter-13-protecting-green-belt-land-gb1-8#gb7-development-which-is-not-inappropriate-in-the-green-belt) (relating to land around well-connected stations) only apply to the area of the site within that reasonable walking distance.
