the decommissioning and removal of existing [renewable and low carbon energy][term:renewable-and-low-carbon-energy] infrastructure on-site, and its replacement with newer, larger or more efficient infrastructure. The repowered site may have a different overall capacity, layout and footprint from the original project. Repowering may require new associated infrastructure such as new network connection infrastructure and foundations.
