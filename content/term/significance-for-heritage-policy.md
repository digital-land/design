The value of a [heritage asset][term:heritage-asset] to this and future generations because of its heritage interest. The interest may be archaeological, architectural, artistic or historic. Significance derives not only from a [heritage asset][term:heritage-asset]’s physical presence, but also from its setting. For World Heritage Sites, the cultural value described within each site’s Statement of [Outstanding Universal Value][term:outstanding-universal-value] forms part of its significance.
