Includes a risk assessment of land potentially affected by contamination, or ground stability and slope stability reports, as appropriate. All assessments of land potentially affected by contamination should be carried out in accordance with established procedures (such as BS10175 Investigation of Potentially Contaminated Sites – Code of Practice).
