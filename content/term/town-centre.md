Area defined in the local authority’s [policies map][term:policies-map] in the development plan including the [primary shopping area][term:primary-shopping-area] and areas predominantly occupied by [main town centre uses][term:main-town-centre-uses] within or adjacent to the [primary shopping area][term:primary-shopping-area]. References to town centres or centres apply to city centres, town centres, district centres and local centres as defined in the development plan but exclude small parades of shops of purely neighbourhood significance. Unless they are identified as centres in the development plan, existing out-of-centre developments, comprising or, including [main town centre uses][term:main-town-centre-uses], do not constitute town centres.
